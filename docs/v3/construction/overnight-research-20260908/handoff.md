## Drift, capacity and time controls

- Checkpoint every 20 minutes or coherent slice, whichever comes first: exact HEAD/dirty state, activation/deadline, current queue item, admitted run IDs/hashes, hypotheses/refutations, residuals, resource state, owned processes, worker deadlines, next command and publication state. Commit coherent slices; an incomplete checkpoint must say incomplete.
- Before every run, delegation, commit and publication, check parent/scope, allowed paths/effects, operational PAUSE, source identity and remaining time/capacity. Reread the active scope hourly, after compaction and after a surprising result. Obtain an independent read-only challenge before scaling or promoting a new conclusion.
- At most four concurrent read-only workers within ten active agents total; twenty minutes per packet; no nested delegation or shared-worktree writes. Astra writes all changes. At most one heavy gate and one owned model process concurrently. Interrupt expired packets and retain their incomplete dispositions.
- Each new model experiment is bounded by 500,000 states per representation, 600 seconds, 2 GiB and 256 MiB artifacts, with model network access disabled. Implement resource admission and enforcement before runs; these numbers do not themselves provide an OS sandbox. Primary-source browsing is separate authorized research. Frozen gates retain their existing checks and need bounded command timeouts that fit the remaining envelope. Only stop processes owned by this task.
- Check every available binding usage window at least every 15 minutes and before expensive runs, workers or publication. Begin finalization at 10% remaining; start no workers at 6%; checkpoint and stop at 3%. Begin earlier if observed drain or gate runtimes threaten the reserve. Missing capacity telemetry or a usage error admits no new expensive work until verified. Never spend credits, change models/tiers or wait for a reset to resume automatically.
- End discovery no later than T0+9h; reserve at least three hours for synthesis, final gates, reviews and publication. Repairs may use the reserve within the same scope and deadline. Stop earlier when evidence converges. Do not add work merely to fill twelve hours.
- At the hard deadline or a binding capacity/boundary stop, leave truthful local state and a handoff. Do not weaken a gate or publish an unfinished candidate as completed. Already launched commands must have finite deadlines; quota exhaustion may prevent a final model response, so earlier durable checkpoints matter.
