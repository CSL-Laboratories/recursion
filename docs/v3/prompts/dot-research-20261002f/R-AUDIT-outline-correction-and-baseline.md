# R-AUDIT — Outline correction and baseline

This handoff is a **correction and verification lane** for the D-039 language-agnostic architecture
outline, responding to the independent static review (GLM-5.3-Flash) that returned **NEEDS
REVISIONS**. Its job is to establish, from primary sources, the correct facts behind the disputed
outline claims (A1–A10), to inventory the strongest existing behavior corpus for the reconstruction
criteria, and to produce the R-LANG-informed revision checklist.
